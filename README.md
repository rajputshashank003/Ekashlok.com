# 🌟 Gita Daily — Timeless Wisdom, Delivered Daily

> **Tech Stack:** Go (Golang) • Gin Framework • React • TypeScript • Tailwind CSS • PostgreSQL • Twilio WhatsApp API • Google OAuth 2.0

---

## 🕊️ Bring the Bhagavad Gita to Your Daily Routine

In the hustle of modern life, finding time for spiritual reflection can be challenging. **Gita Daily** bridges this gap by bringing the timeless, transformative wisdom of the *Bhagavad Gita* directly into your life—**every single morning, right on your WhatsApp**. 

No heavy volumes to carry, no extra apps to clutter your phone, and no reminders to set. Just one profound verse, its translation, and practical commentary delivered to you at **6:00 AM IST** to start your day with clarity, peace, and purpose.

---

## ✨ Key Features Built For Your Spiritual Journey

### 📲 Morning Inspiration, Delivered Directly
*   **Daily WhatsApp Broadcasts:** Wake up to a handpicked shlok sent directly to your chat at **6:00 AM IST** every day.
*   **Zero Friction:** Read, reflect, and digest the wisdom of the day within seconds, directly from your favorite messaging app.

### 📊 Beautiful Progress Tracking
*   **700 Verses, 18 Chapters:** Walk through the complete Gita at your own pace.
*   **Interactive Progress Bar:** Watch your spiritual milestones grow with a clean visual progress tracker on your web dashboard.
*   **Never Lose Your Spot:** Your current reading progress is automatically saved to your profile and synced with your daily WhatsApp deliveries.

### ⚙️ Complete Control Over Your Pace
*   **Custom Reading Milestones:** Want to jump to a specific chapter or verse? The dashboard lets you input any custom shlok number (1–700) to match your current offline study.
*   **Reset Anytime:** Easily restart your journey from Shlok #1 with a single tap.

### 🔐 Secure & Seamless Experience
*   **One-Click Google Authentication:** Sign up securely in seconds. No complicated passwords to remember.
*   **OTP-Verified WhatsApp Subscription:** Secure your daily delivery by verifying your phone number with a quick, high-speed WhatsApp OTP.
*   **Hassle-Free Opt-out:** Unsubscribe instantly with a single button if you need to pause.

## 📝 How It Works

```
[ Step 1: Sign In ] ➔ [ Step 2: Subscribe & Verify ] ➔ [ Step 3: Grow Daily ]
   Google OAuth         Verify WhatsApp via OTP          Receive 1 Shlok at 6 AM
```

1.  **Sign In:** Log in securely via Google.
2.  **Verify & Subscribe:** Input your WhatsApp number, verify it using the OTP sent to your phone, and you are ready.
3.  **Receive Daily:** Every morning, the next verse in your sequence is dispatched automatically. As you read, your progress advances.
